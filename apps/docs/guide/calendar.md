# Calendar & Hours

Owner → **Calendar** →

- **Timezone** — venue local timezone, saved first.
- **Weekly hours** — pick a day, set open/close, or mark closed with a reason (shown in kiosk).
- **Overrides** — per-date exceptions: closed, open, or pricing-only (weekday/weekend).
- **Close venue now** — force-closes for today; grace period 60 min, active sessions auto-settle.
- Preview panel shows the requested date's schedule before saving.

Kiosk and entry scans enforce these hours automatically.
